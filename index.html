Game data comes from the community mhdb-wilds-data extraction (snapshot ). Element and status values are shown the way the game's equipment screen shows them. Defense and resistance totals add the always-on parts of Defense Boost and the elemental resistance skills; attack and affinity add Attack Boost and Critical Eye. Conditional skills are listed but not added to the numbers. Appraised talismans are random, so enter yours with the "Enter an appraised talisman" button. Attack is shown as the in-game number. Artian Rarity 8 element and status values come from GameWith's Artian weapon pages; Rarity 6–7 Artian values and Gogma reinforcement tiers are community estimates. Kinsect stats come from Kiranico and follow the glaive's kinsect level.
